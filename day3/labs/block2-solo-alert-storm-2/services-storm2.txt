FreightFlow EU service dependencies (A -> B means A calls B)

web-portal        -> api-gateway
api-gateway       -> orders-service, inventory-service
orders-service    -> postgres-orders (primary database, 200 GB volume)
inventory-service -> redis-cache (hot stock data)
batch-reports     -> reporting-db (standalone, no other service depends on it)
mail-relay        (standalone, no other service depends on it)
log-server        (standalone, no other service depends on it)
