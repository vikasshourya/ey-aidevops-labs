Before you use AI at work
• Do not paste passwords, keys, customer data or anything confidential into a public AI tool.
• Check your company's AI policy and use the tools it approves.
• AI can be confidently wrong. Always check against the real evidence.
Good questions for your group: What would make you trust the AI's answer? What is the worst that happens if it is wrong? Who should review it? What is the smallest version we can try first?
